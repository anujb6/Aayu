<h2>Overview</h2>

<p><strong>Your fitness fuels a living creature on your wrist.</strong></p>

<p>Exercise more, it grows. Stay consistent, it evolves. Reach the final stage, release it to your collection and start fresh with permanent XP bonuses.</p>

<p>Aayu uses <strong>PAI (Personal Activity Intelligence)</strong> - a heart-rate based health metric - as the energy source for feeding your blob companion. Your workout style shapes its form: runners develop speed affinity, lifters gain power, and endurance athletes get their own unique variant.</p>

<h2>Core Mechanics</h2>

<h3>PAI-Based Feeding</h3>

<p>Unlike step-based apps, Aayu uses PAI - a scientifically-backed metric calculated from your heart rate activity:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Feed Threshold</strong>: Earn 5+ PAI since last feed to unlock feeding</li>
  <li><strong>Multiple Feeds Per Day</strong>: Do multiple workouts? Feed multiple times!</li>
  <li><strong>Delta-Based</strong>: Only new PAI since your last feed counts</li>
</ul>

<p><em>Example: Morning run earns 15 PAI → Feed → Evening gym earns 10 more PAI → Feed again!</em></p>

<h3>Streak Bonuses</h3>

<p>Feed your creature daily to build streaks and earn bonus XP:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>7+ day streak</strong>: +10% XP bonus</li>
  <li><strong>30+ day streak</strong>: +25% XP bonus</li>
</ul>

<h3>Collection & Rebirth System</h3>

<p>Once your creature reaches <strong>Transcendent</strong> (Stage 6), you can release it to your permanent collection:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Ascend to Collection</strong>: Save your fully-evolved creature forever</li>
  <li><strong>Legacy Bonus</strong>: +5% XP per released creature (up to +25% with 5 creatures)</li>
  <li><strong>Fresh Start</strong>: Begin with a new egg while keeping your legacy bonus</li>
  <li><strong>Crown Badge</strong>: Display your release count on the home screen</li>
</ul>

<p>Released creatures are saved with their stats: name, affinities, total XP, days to complete, longest streak, and more.</p>

<h2>Permissions</h2>

<p>The app requires the following permissions:</p>
<ul>
  <li><code>device:os.local_storage</code> - Save creature and collection data</li>
  <li><code>data:user.hd.heart_rate</code> - Heart rate for power affinity</li>
  <li><code>data:user.hd.step</code> - Steps for speed affinity</li>
  <li><code>data:user.hd.distance</code> - Distance for speed affinity</li>
  <li><code>data:user.hd.calorie</code> - Calories for power affinity</li>
  <li><code>data:user.hd.pai</code> - PAI for feeding and endurance affinity</li>
  <li><code>data:user.hd.fat_burning</code> - Fat burn minutes for endurance</li>
</ul>

<h2>How XP is Calculated</h2>

<pre><code>Base XP = PAI earned since last feed
With Legacy = Base XP × (1 + legacyBonus/100)
Final XP = With Legacy × streakMultiplier

Example (3 released creatures, 14-day streak):
  10 PAI × 1.15 (legacy) × 1.10 (streak) = 12.65 → 13 XP</code></pre>
